Why this configuration is chosen:

  • Unfettered Customization: Displays maximum formatting granularity via extensive paragraphStyle, fontFamily selections, and varied specialCharacters ensuring diverse contributor needs are met.
  • Consistent Paste Normalization: Extensively uses wordPasteModal to intercept copied content from varying sources, normalizing team contributions into a unified look.
  • Sticky Readability: Uses toolbarSticky to ensure formatting tools stay glued to the viewport over the lifespan of extremely long, continuously co-authored documents.
